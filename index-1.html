About Obey Your Needs

Return, Refund & Quality Guarantee

Straightforward terms, because you're trusting us with fresh food.

Our quality guarantee

If anything arrives spoiled, damaged, or not what you ordered, message us on WhatsApp within 24 hours of delivery or pickup with a photo — we'll replace the item or refund it, no argument.

Perishable items

Because fresh, frozen, and live items can't be resold once they leave our hands, we can't accept returns for a change of mind after delivery or pickup. Please double-check your order before checkout.

Live animals

For live chicken and turkey, please inspect at the point of delivery or pickup — issues must be raised immediately at handover, as we can't accept a live-animal complaint raised afterward.

Cancellations

You can cancel free of charge any time before we've packed your order. Once it's packed or out for delivery, cancellation may no longer be possible since the items are already committed.

Refunds

Approved refunds are sent back to your original payment method — bank transfer refunds are processed within 5–7 business days. If you paid on delivery, we'll agree the best way to refund you directly.

Delivery windows

At checkout you'll choose a delivery window — 9am–12pm, 12pm–3pm, or 3pm–6pm. We'll do our best to arrive within it, but Lagos traffic means we can't guarantee an exact time.

Delivery issues

If nobody's available to receive a delivery, message us to reschedule — a second delivery attempt carries the same delivery fee as your original order.

Public holidays

Around major public holidays, delivery windows may be more limited or orders may need to be rescheduled — we'll always confirm with you directly rather than leave you guessing.
